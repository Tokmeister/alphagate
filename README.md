# AlphaGate

Autonomous trading intelligence and paper-trading platform.

## Phase 1

- TradingView signal intake
- Market-data integrity gates
- Session VWAP and relative-volume research
- AlphaGate scoring and risk gates
- Paper execution and exit management
- Automated scanner loop
- E2E safety tests

## Safety

TradingView signals are inputs, never direct orders. Live IBKR execution remains disabled during development and validation.
